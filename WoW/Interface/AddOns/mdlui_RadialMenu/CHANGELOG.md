# mdlui_RadialMenu 0.68.0

- **Categories:** six tiles at the top of the editor's sidebar: **Combat**, **Healing**, **Social**, **Group**, **Items** and **Travel & UI**, each with its glyph and count. They start empty and fill only as you choose: drag a radial onto a tile, or right-click a radial for **Move to …**. Click a tile to list only its radials (the rest under **Not filed**, ready to drag in); click it again for all of them. Right-click one to **Empty** it.
- With no tile chosen, the categories with radials in them head the list and fold like your own groups. A shared key still opens the radial highest in the list, so radials in a category come first. Categories can't be renamed, moved or deleted.
- **My radials | Presets:** a switch over the list. Presets are listed under the same category names (Markers under Group, the emotes under Social, Camping and Consumables under Items, Totem Bars under Combat), and the tiles narrow them too. A radial you add, make or import switches back to My radials.
- A radial's key is in a small box at its row's right, and mouse buttons are short (**ALT-M3** for Alt and the middle button), so long keys no longer cut the name off.
- **Every radial** moved to the sidebar's foot.

# mdlui_RadialMenu 0.67.0

- **Consumables preset:** four buttons that fill themselves from your bags: **Health**, **Mana**, **Food** and **Drink**. Each shows the best one you carry; turn the mouse wheel on it to step through the rest, best first. Anything above your level is left out, and a button is gone while you carry none of its kind.
- Health and mana count the potions, healthstones, mana gems and runes by what they restore (Classic's figures). Food and drink are any item eaten or drunk, by the amount on its tooltip.
- The four are also in the Catalog's Items, after Camp Kits.

# mdlui_RadialMenu 0.63.0

- **Camping preset:** a slice for each camp kit you carry, campfires first (the best first), then the camp objects by profession and tier, and **Sit**: a minute sat by the fire gives the camp's full buff.
- **Camp Kits:** the button behind it, also in the Catalog's Items after Quest Items. It finds the kits in your bags by the camp objects' names, and the kits MedalUI has read from your recipes; it is gone while you carry none.
- **Class presets:** a preset for one class, shown only to that class; its copy is for all that class's characters. The first is the shaman's **Totem Bars**: a slice for each of Call of the Elements, of the Ancestors and of the Spirits (each once learned), named and pictured as the Call. Click it or turn the wheel on it and its ring opens: the Call, to drop all four at once, and the four totems on its page of the totem bar, each cast on its own.
- **Totem:** the button behind them, one slot of the totem bar read live: change a totem on Blizzard's bar and the radial follows. An empty slot leaves no button.
- A category can stand for a spell: its name and icon (until you rename it or pick a glyph), and gone while the spell isn't known.

# mdlui_RadialMenu 0.49.0

- **Presets:** ready-made radials in the editor's sidebar, read-only: **Markers**, **Emotes** and **Voice Emotes**. **Add to my radials** makes a copy to change however you like; its Radial sheet's **Reset...** puts it back as it comes, keeping its name and key. The first-run Markers radial counts as the preset's copy.
- **Categories:** a button that holds a ring of its own buttons, kept in its radial. Click it, or turn the mouse wheel on it, and its ring opens in place while you hold the key; let go over a button to use it, and right-click to go back. In click mode a left-click opens it too. Make one from the Catalog's Radials, open it on the editor's wheel to fill it, and give it a name and one of nine glyphs.
- The emote presets group the emotes by feeling: Greetings, Joy, Sorrow, Taunts and At Ease; and for voice emotes Greetings, Playful, Battle, Distress and Signals. Battle's cry is For the Alliance or For the Horde, as your character is.
- The rest of the voice emotes are in the Catalog: Welcome, Congratulate, Flirt, Silly, Train, Rasp, Open Fire, Wait, Nod, No, and your faction's cry.
- Bags opens on a key with a modifier (a radial on Shift-E).

# mdlui_RadialMenu 0.48.0

- First version: FlareUI 6.8.2's radial menu as a MedalUI companion. Radials, keys, click mode, sub-radials and the controller mode work; an editor window (`/mdlradial`, or a key binding) builds them, with an Options tab and an import of FlareUI's radials.
- The wheel in MedalUI's look: FlareUI's layout with its own gold rims (brighter, with a glow, on the aimed button), an ink backdrop with a thin gold ring, a gold centre ring, pointer and cancel X, and names in MedalUI's text colour.
- MedalUI's options have a **Radial menu (ext)** page under Extensions: buttons to the editor and its Options tab.
