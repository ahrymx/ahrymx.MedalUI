# mdlui_RadialMenu 0.48.0

- First version: FlareUI 6.8.2's radial menu as a Medalluin companion. Radials, keys, click mode, sub-radials and the controller mode work; an editor window (`/mdlradial`, or a key binding) builds them, with an Options tab and an import of FlareUI's radials.
- The wheel in Medalluin's look: FlareUI's layout with its own gold rims (brighter, with a glow, on the aimed button), an ink backdrop with a thin gold ring, a gold centre ring, pointer and cancel X, and names in Medalluin's text colour.
- Medalluin's options have a **Radial menu (ext)** page under Extensions: buttons to the editor and its Options tab.
